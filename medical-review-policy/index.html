Medical Review Policy

The scope and limits of medical review for informational wellness content published on this website.

Updated June 2026

Medical review, when applied, is used to improve clarity and factual framing for general informational content. It does not turn an article into personal medical advice.

Scope of review

A medical review process may check whether language is balanced, whether high-risk medical claims are avoided, and whether the page stays within an informational scope.

Review is intended to reduce ambiguity and improve safety-minded wording for readers.

What medical review does not do

Medical review does not create a diagnosis, prescribe a treatment plan, or guarantee outcomes. It also does not replace instructions found on official product packaging or professional advice.

  • No individual evaluation is provided on this website.
  • No claim of cure, prevention, or disease treatment is made.
  • Readers should consult licensed professionals for personal concerns.
