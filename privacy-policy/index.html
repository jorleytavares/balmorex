Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect data across this website.

Updated June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected when you use this website, how that information may be used, and what choices may be available to you.

Information we may collect

We may collect standard website usage data such as page views, device type, browser details, scroll depth, and outbound click activity. If you contact us directly, we may also receive the information you choose to provide.

How information may be used

Collected information may be used to improve website performance, understand engagement, maintain security, and measure article usefulness. We may also use data to understand whether a reader clicked an external offer link.

Cookies and third-party tools

This website may use cookies or similar technologies for analytics and tag management. Third-party services may process data under their own policies when enabled by the publisher.

Your choices

You may limit cookies through your browser settings. If you contact us regarding privacy, we will review the request in line with applicable requirements and practical site operations.
