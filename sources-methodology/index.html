Sources and Methodology

How we choose source material, organize editorial research, and build readable content for older American readers.

Updated June 2026

Our research workflow is designed to prioritize understandable, factual, and well-organized information for adults who want a calmer reading experience.

Source selection

We look for reputable, publicly accessible material such as manufacturer information, product labels, major health organization guidance, and broad educational references about mobility and healthy aging.

Sources are selected for relevance and readability. They are not presented as a substitute for individualized medical guidance.

How articles are built

Each article is organized around plain-language questions readers often ask. We use short paragraphs, clear section headings, and FAQ expansions so the page is easier to scan on mobile devices and understandable for generative search systems.

We also map important entities such as mobility, daily activity, joint comfort, and topical application so the page remains semantically coherent.

Limits of methodology

Our methodology supports informative editorial content, not diagnosis or treatment decisions. Readers should verify ingredients, directions, and safety information on official product resources and speak with a qualified clinician when needed.
